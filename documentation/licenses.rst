Licenses
========

Overview
--------

KivyDK is released and distributed under the terms of the `MIT license <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT_License>`_.

You should have received a copy of the MIT license alongside your KivyDK distribution.
The `license <#kivydk-license>`_ is also available.

In a nutshell, the license allows you to use KivyDK in your own projects regardless of whether they are open source,
closed source, commercial or free. Even if the license doesn't require it, we would really appreciate when you
make changes to the KivyDK sourcecode **itself** and share those changes with us.

~~~~

Responsibility
--------------

Since KivyDK builds upon the Kivy framework and may include other external dependencies, please make sure your own
projects respect the licenses of all included dependencies. Kivy provides a helpful overview of its licensing
terms and recommendations here: `Package licensing <https://kivy.org/doc/stable/guide/licensing.html>`_
